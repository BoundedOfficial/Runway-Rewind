# Runway Rewind

Runway Rewind is a guided makeover game. Its first level takes one character through a sequence of skincare, makeup, and hair treatments.

## Language

**Level**:
A complete makeover experience for a character, comprising an ordered sequence of treatments and the finished result.

**Treatment**:
One stage of the makeover, such as cleansing, applying lipstick, or styling hair. Level 1 contains thirteen treatments.
_Avoid_: Level when referring to a single treatment.

**Tool**:
The implement the player uses to perform a treatment. Tools arrive on the game stage in sequence as the makeover progresses.

**Tool arrival**:
The smooth entrance of the next treatment's tool from outside the visible game stage into view.

**Game stage**:
The visible space containing the character, treatment tools, and gameplay feedback.
_Avoid_: Web page when describing the playable experience.

**Replay**:
Starting Level 1's makeover again from the original dirty state.

**Next**:
The action offered after a finished makeover to continue beyond the current level. Until another level is available, it leads to the end-of-content screen.

**End-of-content screen**:
The screen explaining that more makeovers are coming when the player has finished the available content.
